# Benchmark Setup

![Benchmark network: 64 input spike channels drive 2,048 recurrent COBA-LIF hidden neurons, which drive eight COBA-LIF readout neurons. Hidden and readout spikes are declared outputs; all projections use AMPA synapses.](/streaming-benchmark/streaming-198c79bb6d42/network-2048.png)

This benchmark measures how quickly a recurrent spiking network processes a
continuous stream of input spikes as its size increases. Independent Poisson
spikes arrive on 64 input channels at 25 Hz per channel, drive a recurrent
population of conductance-based leaky integrate-and-fire (COBA-LIF) neurons, and
produce spikes from eight readout neurons. The hidden population ranges from
64 to 2,048 neurons. Weights are randomly initialized and remain fixed throughout
inference; no training is performed.

# Results

![Two panels: callback mean, p95 and p99 versus 64 through 2,048 hidden neurons, crossing the dashed 1 ms budget at 2,048; deadline miss and callback overrun counts, reaching 25,000 each at 2,048.](/streaming-benchmark/streaming-198c79bb6d42/streaming.png)

- A **callback** generates input spikes, advances the network by **1 ms of simulated
time**, and extracts the output spikes.
- Callbacks are scheduled every **1 ms of
wall time**, with a **deadline** 1 ms after each scheduled start.
- Durations below the dashed 1 ms line fit the realtime processing budget, provided the callback
starts on time.
- A **callback overrun** takes longer than 1 ms; a **deadline miss**
finishes after its deadline, which can also happen when a shorter callback starts
late.

The left panel shows the mean, 95th percentile (p95) and 99th percentile
(p99) of callback duration; the right counts individual misses and overruns.
The two counts overlap, and a p99 below 1 ms does not rule out rare failures.
